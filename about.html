About FILEX TRONICS

Tech you can order like a conversation

FILEX TRONICS is a phone and electronics store in Kariakoo, Dar es Salaam, built around WhatsApp. You see the range on this site, choose what you want, and talk to a real person who confirms the price, the stock and how you will receive it.

The range starts with iPhones and extends to Android phones, everyday electronics and the accessories that go with them.
